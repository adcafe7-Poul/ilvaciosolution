1. What is Peekaboo Piano?

When an animal friend pops up out of a hole — peekaboo! — tap it. Its note sounds and the song moves forward one note at a time. Play it like whack-a-mole, and before you know it you have played a whole Beyer piece to the end.

It is made for children and grown-ups to use together. Children play it as a game; grown-ups listen to pieces with the score, the roll and an 88-key keyboard. It is designed for tablets; on a phone, turn it sideways.

2. First launch

When you open the app, you will see Tap to begin. Tap it once and the piano sound gets ready (a few seconds). Browsers and phones can only make sound after a person has tapped once, which is why this button is there.

3. Home screen

  • Four bookshelf tabs — Beyer Czerny Op.599 Czerny Op.849 Carols & Folk.
  • Song cards — songs marked 🐾 and Play can be played with the friends; Listen songs are for listening. Songs marked 🔒 open with Pro (section 11).
  • Selected song — its name and the opening of its score appear on the right.
  • Nine theme buttons — tap a friend's face and the game starts right away in that theme.
  • Play with friends Listen/Practice — on a phone in landscape there is less room, so Play with friends is hidden and the theme buttons do its job.

4. Play with friends

The holes in the bottom row are the white keys, and the holes in the top row are the black keys. The note name is written under each hole.

  • Tap the glowing friend — the friend whose turn it is glows and peeks out. Tap the right one and you move on to the next note. Mistakes are fine — it waits until you tap the right friend.
  • Left Both Right — choose which hand plays. The app plays the hand you did not choose as accompaniment.
  • One key is enough — when it is on, tapping just one note of a chord makes the other notes sound by themselves. Good for little children.
  • Practice — no song; every friend peeks out. Tap as much as you like. Tap it again to go back to the song game.
  • Hear it first — listen to the song first. Tapping it turns the button into ■ Stop, and the song repeats until you stop it. Each time a round ends, «Playing again from the start» shows for a moment. Tap ■ Stop to go back to the start of the song game. From the start — start the song again from the beginning.
  • Dynamics — at 0 every note is equally loud; turned all the way up, the notes follow the loudness written in the score. Volume — how loud the sound is.
  • Notes hit — under the song name you will see something like «Notes 5 / 17».
  • When you finish — a celebration bursts out and a sparkling theme friend's face (Once more) appears. Tap it to play again from the beginning.
  • Changing the theme while playing — tap the button at the top right of the stage: only the background and the friends change at once, and the song carries on.
  • At the top, 4–8 bars of the score of the song you are playing are shown, and a red line follows where you are.

5. Sweep play (glissando)

Keep your finger on the holes and slide it sideways: the notes of the holes you pass sound one after another. The notes you have passed do not stop at once; they ring a little longer (0.6 s), so it feels like sweeping across a real piano. It also works on the keyboard in Listen/Practice.

When you sweep along the top row (the black keys), only the top row sounds even if your finger sags a little — just as a pianist sweeping the black keys does not touch the white ones. Pressing between two black holes plays the nearer black key.

6. Listen/Practice

  • Ready screen — at first the roll and the keyboard are empty, with a big ▶ in the middle. Tap it and the notes fall from the top and sound when they reach the keys. When the song ends, you come back to the ready screen.
  • From top to bottom, the score · scrolling roll · 88-key keyboard move together as the song plays. The right hand is teal and the left hand is orange.
  • Listen with Play · Pause, the position bar and Speed.
  • You can also press the 88 keys yourself to play along.
  • Repeat Play next — in Listen mode. Repeat plays the same song again and again until you stop it; Play next moves on to the next song in bookshelf order. Tap the same button again to turn it off, and the song plays once. Your choice is remembered.
  • Switch modes with the Listen Practice buttons at the top. Your choice is remembered.
  • Practice shows a keyboard sized to the song: the smallest real keyboard (37, 49, 61, 76 or 88 keys) that holds every note, with its size in the bar, such as "49 keys · C2–C6". You can tell at once whether your own keyboard is big enough.
  • In Practice, notes fall straight down onto their keys, with bar lines and bar numbers, at the same speed throughout the song.

8. Menu lock — for parents

Press and hold the key button at the top left for 3 seconds to lock the menus (a silver padlock). Even if a child taps other buttons while playing, the screen will not change. The holes on the stage and the keys in Listen/Practice can still be played. Hold for 3 seconds again to unlock. The lock stays on even after you reopen the app.

10. Song list

  • Beyer: 106 pieces (83 to play) · Czerny Op.599: 100 pieces (28 to play) · Czerny Op.849: 30 pieces (2 to play)
  • Four carols (Jingle Bells · Silent Night · Deck the Halls · We Wish You a Merry Christmas)
  • Nine folk songs (Arirang · Ongheya · Kwaejina Chingching Nane · Monggeumpo Taryeong · Hangangsu Taryeong · Nilliriya · Han Obaengnyeon · Baennorae · Shark Campfire Song)

Every score was newly entered from out-of-copyright original editions, and the carols and folk songs are this app's own two-hand arrangements.

11. Free edition and Pro

  • Free edition — the Forest, Sea and Jungle themes, 23 play songs (the first 20 Beyer pieces + Jingle Bells · Arirang · Shark Campfire Song), and Listen and Practice for all 249 pieces.
  • Pro lifetime pass (one-time payment) — all nine themes · all 126 play songs.
  • Tap a song or theme marked 🔒 and the «Opens with Pro» window appears. Tap For grown-ups and a Grown-up check question (multiplying two numbers) appears; only a correct answer leads on to Google Play payment.
  • If you have changed phones, tap Restore purchase in ⓘ About (with the same Google account).

12. Big screens · interactive whiteboards

On Android touch TVs and interactive whiteboards, install the app. On whiteboards connected to a Windows PC, you can use the web edition package (double-click the start file and it opens only on that PC). Several fingers can press at the same time.

13. Privacy · contact

No sign-up and no ads. No information is sent anywhere. The app goes online only to connect to Google Play when you make a purchase.

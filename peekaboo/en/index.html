Peekaboo Piano

& Friends

Tap the friends that pop up and play the piano.

A piano you play like a whack-a-mole game. No sheet music needed: follow the glowing friends and you will play a whole piece from the classic Beyer piano method. Made for children and grown-ups together.

Coming soon to Google Play User manual

What you can do

🐾 Play with friends

The friend whose turn it is glows. Tap the right one to move on — the game waits for you. Right, left or both hands.

🎹 Like a real piano

Recorded grand piano, electric piano, marimba or synth. Slide a finger for a glissando.

🎨 Nine themes

Forest · Sea · Jungle · Stage · AI robots on the Moon · Crystal forest · Halloween · Dinosaurs · Snowland. Switch anytime.

🎼 Songs

Beyer 106 · Czerny Op. 599, 100 · Op. 849, 30 · 13 carols and folk songs.

🎧 Listen · Practice

Score, falling roll and 88 keys move together. Practice drops notes straight onto a keyboard sized to the song (37–88 keys).

🔑 Menu lock

Hold the key for 3 seconds to lock the menus while a child plays.

Today's Beat S

Korean Version

One tap, a new drum beat!

A dice-rolling drum playground made of 4-bar blocks. No sheet music, no drumming skills needed: tap, shake and roll to make your own beat.

Get it on Google Play Manual

What you can do

🥁 The gold button

A new beat every tap. Each 4-bar block picks an engine and a tempo, with fills and breakdowns.

🎼 Seven meters

4/4 · 3/4 · 2/4 · 6/8 · 12/8 · 5/4 · 7/8, each with its own signature genres.

🎻 Inspiration Drop

Originals, classics and Korean folk songs play along with your beat.

📳 Shake to swap kits

Ten drum kits, including a Korean Percussion kit built from the National Gugak Center's recordings.

🌧 Ambient FX

Eleven sounds, from rain and waves to forest, campfire and space, under your beat.

🎮 Beat game · 💃 Dancers

Hit the keys as the rolls fall, while neon dancers move to the beat.

Free and Pro

Free to download

  • Gold-button beats without limit
  • 6 tunes and four engines
  • Edit bar 1 (songs are not saved)

✨ Pro lifetime pass

  • One-time purchase inside the app through Google Play
  • All 80 tunes · edit every bar
  • House, Eurodance and K-pop engines
  • Record · Open · MIDI Save · Save work

Stays on your device

No sign-up and no ads. Songs and settings are stored only on your device, and the app works offline (it connects to Google Play only when you make a purchase). The app speaks eight languages: 한국어 · English · 日本語 · 中文 · Монгол · Español · Português.
